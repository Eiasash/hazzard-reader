# Chapter 86 Study: Hepatic, Pancreatic, and Biliary Diseases

## Summary

**Liver aging & injury**
- Liver tests and coagulation factors generally do not change with age; functional hepatocyte volume and hepatic blood flow decline (p1337–1338).
- Phase I metabolism of some drugs falls by **as much as 50%**; phase II is relatively preserved. Start low and titrate slowly (p1338).
- Drug-induced liver injury is more frequent and severe in older adults; review prescription and nonprescription exposure (p1342).
- Metabolic syndrome raises fatty-liver risk. Alcohol abstinence can improve alcohol-associated disease even after decompensation (p1339–1340).

**Chronic disease & malignancy**
- PBC: cholestatic tests, antimitochondrial antibody; ursodeoxycholic acid slows progression. Autoimmune hepatitis requires biopsy; immunosuppression is often effective (p1342).
- PSC: evaluate with cholangiography, usually MRCP; manage cholangitis/strictures and monitor for cholangiocarcinoma (p1342–1343).
- Ascites, encephalopathy and frailty complicate cirrhosis care. Search for triggers of each encephalopathy episode; balance symptom benefit against treatment burden (p1343).
- Transplantation can suit selected older adults; age alone is not a contraindication (p1343).

**Biliary disease**
- Acute cholecystitis may lack fever or marked pain. Ultrasound is the initial investigation; ERCP is therapeutic for common-duct stones (p1345).
- Incidental asymptomatic gallstones do not require treatment. Early laparoscopic surgery suits appropriate patients; drainage may suit very ill patients (p1346).

**Pancreas**
- Aging may dilate pancreatic ducts without pancreatic disease; malignancy becomes more frequent with age (p1348).
- Acute pancreatitis: pain with amylase/lipase typically **at least 3×** normal; lipase remains elevated longer (p1348).
- Without gallstones or substantial alcohol exposure, consider underlying malignancy; check triglycerides early (p1348).
- Chronic pancreatitis: pain, malabsorption and diabetes; enzyme replacement treats malabsorption, and normal enzymes do not exclude disease (p1349).
- Pancreatic cancer needs staging for vascular invasion/metastases. Mucinous cysts/main-duct IPMN may warrant surgery in patients fit enough; branch-duct lesions may undergo surveillance (p1349–1350).
