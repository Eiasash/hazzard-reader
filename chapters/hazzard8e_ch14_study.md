# Chapter 14 Study: Models of Hospital and Outpatient Care

## Summary

**Choose a model that fits**
- Define the local problem, population, patient goals, team and payment environment; measure baseline outcomes and implementation fidelity (p191-194).
- Models aim to preserve function and quality of life in the least restrictive safe setting; sustaining and spreading them requires leadership, resources and outcome feedback (p191, p203-204).

**Hospital and perioperative care**
- ACE combines a supportive environment, interdisciplinary assessment, functional care and early discharge planning; ACE consultation applies its principles without a dedicated unit (p194-197).
- HELP addresses cognition, sleep, mobility, vision, hearing and hydration; the described target is age **70+** with at least one delirium risk factor (p198).
- NICHE develops nursing expertise through geriatric resource nurses and evidence-based care processes (p199).
- Hospital VA STRIDE provides supervised walking after early assessment; selection excludes patients for whom the protocol is unsuitable (p199).
- AGS CoCare: Ortho co-manages hip fractures; GSV sets geriatric surgical standards; POSH coordinates geriatrics, surgery and anesthesia for elective surgery (p200).

**Community and home models**
- GRACE combines primary care with nurse-practitioner/social-worker support for low-income older adults (p200-201).
- PACE requires age **55+**, state-certified nursing-home eligibility, residence in the service area and ability to remain safely in the community with services (p201).
- STEADI screens, assesses modifiable fall risks and intervenes; outpatient STRIDE is a separate nurse-led fall-injury prevention model (p202).
- Home-Based Primary Care serves severe disabling chronic illness; Independence at Home targets high-risk, high-cost patients with limited mobility and coordinated longitudinal care (p202-203).
- CAPABLE combines occupational therapy, nursing and home repairs to improve function and support aging in place (p203).
- UCLA dementia care uses specialist-supported nurse-practitioner co-management to support patients and caregivers; Gerofit offers exercise for eligible older veterans (p203).
- Fit services to patient abilities and exclusion criteria; similar program names do not imply identical populations or evidence (p199, p202-203).
- Sustain the model with an advisory team including a patient, outcome reporting, staff development and plans for dissemination (p204).
