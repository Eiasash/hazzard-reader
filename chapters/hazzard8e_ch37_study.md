# Chapter 37 Study: Sexuality, Sexual Function, and the Aging Man

## Summary

**Assessment and context**
- Many men remain sexually active into late life; changes in health, partners and social circumstances affect activity as well as physiology (p547-549).
- Initiate a respectful discussion and assess the particular concern: erection, desire, performance anxiety or orgasm/ejaculation (p549, p551).
- Explore partner and psychological factors, depression, vascular disease, diabetes and medication effects (p550-551).
- Erectile dysfunction commonly has vascular or neurologic causes; low testosterone is not its universal explanation (p547, p550-551).
- In long-term care, support privacy and intimacy while assessing consent and capacity for the particular situation (p549).

**Treat erectile dysfunction**
- Address lifestyle factors, reversible disease and contributing medicines before direct treatment (p551).
- Sildenafil, vardenafil, tadalafil and avanafil have broadly similar efficacy; all require sexual stimulation (p551-552).
- Sildenafil/vardenafil: take on an empty stomach; food does not significantly impair tadalafil/avanafil absorption. Tadalafil lasts longer (p552).
- These agents are contraindicated with nitrates; caution with alpha blockers because of hypotension. Headache, flushing and dyspepsia are common (p552).
- Intracavernosal therapy or urethral alprostadil are alternatives; local pain, prolonged erections or fibrosis are relevant cautions (p552).
- Vacuum devices and prostheses offer nonoral options. Do not leave the constriction ring on beyond **30 minutes**; use care with anticoagulation (p552).
- Prosthesis infection is an important complication; treatment choice should account for acceptability and practical use (p552).

**Testosterone**
- Diagnose hypogonadism from compatible symptoms/signs plus consistently low morning, preferably fasting, levels on at least two occasions (p552).
- Testosterone can modestly improve desire, activity and erectile function in hypogonadal men; it does not improve these outcomes in eugonadal men (p553).
- Adding testosterone after failure of an oral erectile-dysfunction drug may help severe hypogonadism, not normal testosterone status (p553).
- The chapter directs detailed testosterone evaluation and prescribing to the endocrine chapter; it supplies no universal dose here (p552-553).
